# Report fields

Reference for reading `report.json` and `report.html`. Steps live in [the skill](../SKILL.md).

## Views and input coverage

Structure contains Anatomy and Pace. Objective groups belong to the Course trace view, which view links call `trace`; its Table mode lists all members, where each objective is stated and what it builds toward.

Schema 1's text projection is partial. Earlier schema 1 output has no correctness or scoring; projection 2 supplies authored correctness and scoring separately. Narration times are script estimates; stored sidecar endpoints don't establish full-file duration. Studio refs are snapshot addresses, so find content through Trace source pointers and the lesson line ranges. Schema 0, from older Studio builds' plain `praxity inspect <course-dir>`, is still accepted.

## Objective levels

`alignment.objectives[]` carries `bloomCandidates` (every listed level of the leading verb), `verbBloom` (the listed level when there is exactly one, otherwise null) and `modelBloom` (the reviewer's level from the verb and its object, or null). `bloomDiffers` is true only when a single listed level and a non-null reviewer level differ; `levelReason` records why. Each objective's `ref` resolves to its source pointer in `alignment.places`.

`bloom` keeps the single listed level as the measurement. For multi-level or unlisted verbs it uses `modelBloom`, marked generated by `bloomSource`. Read a difference as a prompt to inspect the objective's object and the reviewer's reasoning, not as a score.

## Transcripts

Transcript lines are supplied media prose, including descriptions with no speech. Projection 2 supplies authored `media.transcript`; caption-track contents stay unavailable. Schema 0 and embedded Studio HTML may supply `data.transcript`, and HTML input also reads local VTT caption and subtitle tracks. Transcripts add no on-screen words, narration time or playback time.

`language.lessons[].channels.transcript` holds transcript sentence and word summaries, and English vocabulary counts include `language.vocabulary.lessons[].transcript`. Both are absent only when the course has no transcript text. Transcript sentences use `channel: "transcript"` and ids such as `3.9.t2`, with the owning media block's ref.

Terms flags, Tasks evidence and Visuals opportunities accept `screen`, `narration` or `transcript`, and each quote must occur in its cited channel. Alignment support also accepts `tooltip`. `availability.checks[].available` can be `transcript`, labelled "In a transcript only".

## Item cues

Only single-selection choices with exactly one known key and at least two readable text options are included, even when unscored. Read `excluded` and `excludedCount`; each reason names the field, tag, attribute or bracket to fix or check by hand. Trace excludes an item when:

- its HTML uses markup beyond attribute-free `p`, `strong`, `em`, `code`, `del` and `mark`; `a` with only `href`; tooltips with exactly `class="inline-tooltip-trigger"` and `data-tooltip-content`; and doodles with exactly `class="praxity-doodle"`, `data-doodle` and `data-doodle-mods`;
- an icon or variable placeholder hides what the learner sees;
- its question or options still contain square or curly brackets once glossary definitions are removed, even brackets meant as text.

`itemCues.excludedCount` retains its existing meaning: choices excluded from all
probes. It equals `itemCues.course.excludedCount`, `excluded.length`, and the sum
of `banks[].excludedCount`. `count` still counts structurally included choices,
even when unscored. Course `count` equals the sum of bank counts.

Every course and bank row also has `excludedByReason`, with every stable code
below present as a nonnegative integer, including zeros, and `maskedExcludedCount`.
The latter counts structurally included items withheld from masked preparation;
it does not change `count` or the plain/cover denominators. One language or
mask-space failure withholds every included item in that bank, so each receives
the bank's masked reason. Eligible banks remain runnable even when pooled course
scoring is withheld. Course `maskedExcludedCount` equals the bank sum. For each row:

- `sum(excludedByReason)` equals `excludedCount + maskedExcludedCount`.
- The candidate inventory is `count + excludedCount` distinct choices.
- The masked runnable inventory is `count - maskedExcludedCount`.

Each structurally excluded record carries `code`, human-readable `reason`, one
counting owner `bank`, and `banks` for group-membership provenance. An overlapping
choice counts once under its lesson, retaining all group refs in `banks`; it never
counts twice in the course or bank totals. Groups with no uniquely owned choices
remain provenance rather than extra counting banks. Structural reasons are the
first applicable cause. Structural and masked-only counts are disjoint.

| Stable code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `no-key-or-poll` | Correctness is open/unavailable or no option has a key. Studio cannot distinguish a missing `(x)` from an intentional poll, even with `{scored}`. |
| `multiple-select` | Multiple selection is outside these single-key probes. |
| `insufficient-options` | Fewer than two known options. |
| `key-count` | More than one key on a single-selection item. |
| `ineligible-stem` | Stem or option text has unsupported markup, unresolved brackets or no comparable text. |
| `overlapping-groups` | The choice has ambiguous assessment-group ownership. |
| `masked-language` | Masked-only exclusion: authored English provenance is missing, malformed, defaulted, foreign or conflicts with listed text markers. |
| `masked-space` | Masked-only exclusion: a safe injective mask could not be built. |

Wholly excluded banks remain with `count: 0`, no probe scores and
`masked.reason: "no-included-items"`. Their detail names the structural codes to
review in `excluded`, rather than prescribing an unrelated language change. Such
empty banks do not themselves invalidate scores for other included banks;
source-count reconciliation and exclusion review remain separate gate checks.

Consumers must stop if the writer's declared scored-choice count differs from
Trace's included `course.count` or the corresponding bank count. Trace includes
unscored keyed checks too, so establish the same inventory scope before comparing;
never shrink a declared count to hide a missing key. Treat `multiple-select` and
explicitly declared polls as expected exclusions outside the single-key gate,
recording their refs in the source inventory. A `no-key-or-poll` code alone is
not a poll declaration. Expected exclusions do not waive a count mismatch; resolve
the scope explicitly. Unexpected structural exclusions stop the gate.

Read the existing top-level `itemCues.excludedCount` for the structural course
total. Missing per-row `excludedCount`, `excludedByReason` or `maskedExcludedCount`
means an older Trace cannot supply this accounting: stop and update it, rather
than treating absent fields as zeros. Masked-language or masked-space exclusions
leave the masked gate unavailable; stop and resolve the stated reason.

Tooltip definitions stay out of solver inputs. Schema 1, projection 2 reads the
optional boolean `assessment.shuffle`. `true` means shuffled and `false` means
fixed order. Absence in older Studio output remains unknown. Present nonboolean
values are rejected. Shuffled and shuffle-unknown items keep authored positions
as evidence but are left out of fixed-position counts.
Items counted by `spreads[].unknownCount` are not covered by the position check.
If their sum is positive, warn that coverage is incomplete; a gate requiring
verified shuffle or position coverage must stop, even if every reported fixed
position count is zero.

Each item has a visible `label` with its full block ref and a short stem excerpt, used in the runnable diagnostic result arrays. HTML rows add the source file and line.

Item-cues uses prompt version `item-cues/14-projection-2`. Preparation writes only the masked probe by default and creates a sibling review folder, `<bundle>-review/`. Add `--diagnostics` to also write choices-only and cover-the-options probes. Its `operator.json` alone records `seed` and `optionOrder`, mapping refs to zero-based source indices; solver choices remain one-based. Keep keyed `course.json` in a private input folder outside the bundle's parent tree. Give each solver only its probe folder copied into a separate workspace, or supply the files inline; keep the operator workspace out of reach. The bundle manifest carries `view`, `promptVersion`, `courseHash`, `bundleId` and masked applicability, without seed or order. Each preparation gets a new random 128-bit `bundleId`, independent of the seed; the operator file stores their association. Reusing a seed replays content and option order, not the ID. Each runnable probe's items carry `bundleId` and a `probe` tag. Echo those unchanged at the top of each solver answer. Tags are `masked`, `choices-only` and `cover-the-options`. Combine run probes under `masked`, `choicesOnly` and `coverTheOptions` in `<review>/answer.json`. `masked` is required, including its unavailable status when held. Diagnostics are optional. Omit `choicesOnly` and `coverTheOptions` or set either to `"not run"` when skipped; their report fields are then absent. Diagnostics do not affect masked scores. Report reads `operator.json` beside the answer. Moving the combined answer requires moving its operator file too.

Plain choices-only answer objects have `{bundleId, probe: "choices-only", model, answers}`. Each row has `ref`, one-based `choice`, a supported `cue` code from the prompt and `note`; `other` needs an explanation. Cover-the-options uses the same identity fields, with `ref` and short free `answer` rows. When run, each diagnostic solver answers every supplied item once. Masked prompts and answers exclude `only-true-on-its-own` and `textbook-wording`; plain choices-only retains both subject-knowledge codes. Masked uses `{bundleId, probe: "masked", applies: true, model, answers}` when any banks are runnable, with exactly the refs in masked/items.json. If none are runnable, copy `{bundleId, probe: "masked", applies: false, reason, detail}` from the operator prompt.

Studio schema 1, projection 2 now reports each lesson's `language: {value, source}`. Masked eligibility requires source `lesson` or `course` and an English primary subtag, compared case-insensitively with whitespace trimmed. A lesson source of `course` means an authored manifest declaration. Inspect's workspace context is unavailable; its `default` fallback is never evidence. Missing, malformed or defaulted lesson fields mean `language-unconfirmed`. Add `locale: en` to `course.yaml` or `lang: en` per lesson, use a Studio build with inspect language provenance, then re-run inspect. Declared non-English values and listed text conflicts mean `language`, with affected refs in detail. A value without a valid authored source stays unconfirmed. Evidence stays associated with each lesson and the producer revision already bound by courseHash. Declarations confirm declared language, not every sentence.

The deny-only screen checks each combined stem and options against explicit non-English-only and homograph lists. One non-English-only hit blocks. A homograph needs a second distinct marker in that item, or zero English evidence words in the item; repeated occurrences of the same homograph do not count as a second marker. Markers in different items never reinforce each other. English-discriminating evidence uses a separate explicit list, excluding known homographs and I from evidence while retaining them in masking. NFC letter words, internal apostrophes and case normalization define tokens; digits and punctuation are outside the denominator. Evidence percentages never authorize a score or veto a terse stem. A noun-phrase bank with content-only options stays eligible under authored English unless it triggers those listed-marker rules. The screen catches listed markers only. It is not language identification: when an English declaration is wrong, unlisted foreign words, including foreign options, can score. The declaration is the control; the screen is a backstop.

Every bank's `masked` is explicitly unavailable, eligible but unsolved, or scored. Unavailable objects are `{applies: false, reason, detail}` and omit `right`, `total`, `chanceExpected`, `p`, `pLow`, `pMin`, `pLowMin`, `tooFewItems` and `tooFewItemsLow`. Eligible unsolved objects are `{applies: true, status: "not run"}`. Scored objects add `status: "scored"` to the choice score fields. An unresolved, foreign or unmaskable item keeps its bank and the pooled course unavailable, without dropping that item from `count`. Other banks retain their complete results. The manifest's `maskedBanks` maps bank IDs to eligibility; its pooled `masked` can be unavailable while masked/items.json contains rows for unaffected complete banks. No partial-bank or partial-course scores are emitted. Preparation, parsing and reporting recompute eligibility rather than trusting answer claims. Blocked refs in answers reject, and injected scores are discarded. When the masked gate is unavailable, stop and ask; never compute masked tails by hand or use a hand-masked fallback.

`choicesOnly` keeps its existing score fields, including `right`, `total`, `chanceExpected`, exact `p` and `pLow`, `pMin` and `pLowMin`, `tooFewItems`, `tooFewItemsLow`, `cueCounts` and `items`. Both tails include the observed count and each item's `1/options`, using the exact Poisson-binomial for mixed option counts. It remains the "does the stem matter?" diagnostic, because subject knowledge may identify the only generally true option. Both scored choice probes expose `pMin`, the upper tail with every answer right, and `pLowMin`, the lower tail with every answer wrong, per bank and course. Consumers compare those minima with their own thresholds. Small-bank flags retain a 0.05 reference and mean that tail cannot establish evidence at that reference. Avoid interpreting isolated banks or repeated runs as independent evidence.

The text transform uses one deterministic injective dictionary across each item's NFC-normalized stem and options. Content words become same-length pseudo-words. Silent-h hour, honest, honour/honor and heir families retain vowel onsets. You-sound words use a closed list, including unicorn, university, utility, useful, used, usable, unified, usage, usability, utilize, utilization, usual, Eurozone, euros and European; broad uni-/ur- prefixes are not supported. SMART, SCORM, HIPAA and LAN are explicitly spoken acronyms; MRI, HIV and X-ray use letter names (A E F H I L M N O R S X take an). All-caps replacements start with aeio for vowel sounds or bcdgjkpqtvwyz for consonant sounds, so spelling and word reading agree. This bounded table approximates common pronunciation only. Regular plurals, KPIs, APIs and menu/menus share a base plus trailing `s`; virus, radius, analysis and glass stay whole. English function words, including pronoun I, cue words and punctuation remain. Digits use one consistent scrambled map per item. Outputs never reuse another word's output, equal a kept word or equal a source word. Exhausted forms or residual combining marks withhold the owning bank and pooled course with reason `mask-space`. These rules cannot authorize language eligibility.

`coverTheOptions` keeps every stem, response and key with its judgement or null. Before judgement, `right` is null and `status` is "not judged". Matches and `alsoCorrect` answers both count in `right`, the number of defensible stem answers; `outcomes.alsoCorrect` counts the other valid answers separately. There is no p value for free answers, and answerability is a different question from choices-only cue detection.

`cueCounts` reports cues to the key. `writingCounts` reports high option overlap and shared openings, which may be intentional parallel options. Each pattern has an author action with limits against overcorrection. `choicesOnly.cueCounts` totals solver cue codes; unavailable masked objects have no cue counts.

Convergence is a bank or course statistic against guessing, including exact fractional tie-credit tails. Only a unique key maximum is listed per item, and it is left out of `cueCounts`. Consultation is also a bank share, never an item flag. English lexical rules run only for an `en` locale; other languages show "Not checked: English-only rule" in `applicability`. Unicode duplicate, overlap and shared-lead checks always run. Duplicate detection preserves signs, meaningful symbols, fractions and decimal separators.

`spreads` separates option counts and gives authored positions, known fixed-order positions, shuffled and unknown counts, length ranks and longest-key counts including ties. Length is the visible character count including spaces; rank 1 is shortest and ties share the lower rank. The equal-share reference is items divided by options. The 10 to 40% rank band needs at least 20 items; use the pooled course spread for smaller banks. Spread key length ranks across the bank rather than moving every key to another single rank.
